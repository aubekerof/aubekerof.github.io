Notch Mirror

Your Mac's notch, live on your iPhone.

What's playing, your Mac's battery and whatever you drop on the notch show up in the Dynamic Island, with controls that reach back to your Mac.

Download for Mac iPhone app on the App Store soon

Mac app: free, macOS 14 or later · iPhone app: $3.99, iOS 17.2 or later

What it does

Music in the island

Whatever is playing on your Mac, from a music app to a video in the browser, appears in the Dynamic Island with its artwork. Play, pause and skip from the island, and set your Mac's volume from your iPhone.

Swing to paste

Copy something on your iPhone, open Notch Mirror and flick the top edge away from you: your Mac pastes it where you're typing. Send several at once and one flick pastes them all. Swipe an item on the shelf to paste that one instead.

Drop it on the notch

Drag a file, an image or some text onto the notch and pick it up on your iPhone. Send things back from the iPhone's share sheet. New Mac screenshots can land there too. Select several in the notch and drag them out together.

Your Mac at a glance

Your Mac's battery sits in the island next to the music, and on the Lock Screen when your iPhone is locked. A Bluetooth link keeps it current while the app is closed.

Private by design

Your iPhone and Mac talk directly over your Wi‑Fi and Bluetooth. No account, no tracking, and nothing you send between them goes to a server. You pair them by comparing a code on both screens, and from then on everything between them is encrypted.

Widget and Shortcuts

A Home Screen widget shows the newest item from your Mac. Get Latest from Mac and Send to Mac work in Shortcuts, so you can put them on Back Tap.

Getting started

  1. Get the Mac appDownload Notch Mirror for Mac, move it to Applications and open it. It lives in the notch.
  2. Open it on your iPhoneInstall Notch Mirror from the App Store and open it on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  3. Pair onceCheck that the code in the notch matches the one on your iPhone, then confirm Pair on both.

What you need

iPhone
iOS 17.2 or later. The Dynamic Island needs iPhone 14 Pro or later, except iPhone 16e and iPhone 17e. On other models Notch Mirror shows on the Lock Screen.
Mac
macOS 14 or later. Made for MacBook models with a notch.
Connection
iPhone and Mac on the same Wi‑Fi network, with Bluetooth on.
