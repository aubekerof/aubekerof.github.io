Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

Notch Mirror does not collect personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no ads. Your iPhone and your Mac talk to each other directly, and what they exchange stays on your devices. The developer never receives any of it.

What the app handles

Notch Mirror moves information between your own iPhone and your own Mac:

  • what's playing on your Mac (title, artist, artwork and playback state) and your Mac's battery level;
  • items you choose to send: text, images and files you drop on the notch, share to Notch Mirror or copy with it, and, if you turn this on, new screenshots from your Mac;
  • commands from your iPhone, such as play, pause, skip or volume;
  • if you turn on Swing to paste, what you last copied on your iPhone, every item of it if you copied several at once: it goes to your Mac when you open Notch Mirror, so a swing can paste it at once. The Mac keeps it in memory for five minutes and puts it on the shelf only when you swing. One swing pastes everything you sent from your iPhone since the last swing, up to five minutes apart, until something else is copied on either device. To recognize the swing, the app reads your iPhone's motion sensors while it is open. Those readings stay on the iPhone and are not kept.

Where it goes and where it stays

This information travels only over your local network and Bluetooth, between devices you paired yourself. Once paired, everything they exchange is encrypted.

It is stored only on your devices, in Notch Mirror's own storage on your iPhone and on your Mac. Pairing keys are kept in the Keychain on each device. Each app also keeps a short technical log on its own device for troubleshooting: events, timings and file names, never the contents of what you copy or send. It stays on the device unless you choose to share it, on the iPhone with About → Share the technical log.

Update check on the Mac

The Mac app is not distributed through the App Store, so once a day it downloads a small file from this website to see whether a newer version exists. The request contains no information about you or what you use the app for. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to GitHub, which hosts this site.

Permissions

Each permission is used only for the purpose described here:

  • Local Network and Bluetooth, on iPhone and Mac: to find your other device and talk to it;
  • Notifications, on iPhone: to tell you when an item from your Mac is ready to copy;
  • Full Disk Access, on Mac, optional: to keep photos you copy on your iPhone, which macOS stores in a protected folder;
  • Access to your screenshots, on Mac, only if Add new screenshots is on: to put new screenshots on the shelf;
  • Paste from Other Apps, on iPhone, only for Swing to paste: to read what you copied, so your Mac can paste it;
  • Accessibility, on Mac, only for Swing to paste: to paste into the app you're typing in, the way ⌘V does, and to leave password fields alone.

You can turn any of them off at any time: in Settings on your iPhone, or in System Settings → Privacy & Security on your Mac. Notch Mirror then stops using that access.

Third parties

Notch Mirror shares no data with anyone and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Two Apple features may be involved, and Apple's privacy policy governs them:

  • If you use Universal Clipboard, text that Notch Mirror puts on the clipboard can travel between your devices through it.
  • If you chose to share analytics with app developers on your iPhone, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports.

Keeping and deleting data

The shelf keeps only the newest items (20 by default) and removes older items and their files automatically. You can also:

  • delete items in the app at any time;
  • stop a phone and Mac from talking to each other: on the Mac, open the gear in the notch, then iPhones → Forget;
  • remove the items the app stores on a device by deleting the app from it.

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when you visit; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
