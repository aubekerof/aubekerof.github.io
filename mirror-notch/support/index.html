Support

Something not working, or an idea for Mirror Notch? Write to notchmirrorsupport@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone and Mac models and their system versions. If something went wrong with the connection, attach the iPhone's log too: in Mirror Notch, About → Share the technical log.

Common questions

What do I need?

  • iPhone with iOS 17.2 or later. The Dynamic Island needs iPhone 14 Pro or later, except iPhone 16e and iPhone 17e. On other models Mirror Notch shows on the Lock Screen.
  • Mac with macOS 14 or later, with the free Mirror Notch app for Mac. It's made for MacBook models with a notch.
  • Both devices on the same Wi‑Fi network, with Bluetooth on.

How do I connect my iPhone and Mac?

  1. Download Mirror Notch for Mac, move it to Applications and open it.
  2. Open Mirror Notch on your iPhone, on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  3. The notch on your Mac and your iPhone show a code. Check that the two match, then confirm Pair on both.

Where is the Mac app?

It lives in the notch. Move the pointer up to the notch to open it. There's no Dock or menu bar icon: open the app again from Applications or Spotlight, and the notch shows where it is. Settings are behind the gear in the open notch. To start it with your Mac, turn on Open at login in Settings → General.

How does Swing to paste work?

  1. In Mirror Notch on your iPhone, turn on Swing to paste.
  2. On your Mac, open the gear in the notch and, under Swing to paste, choose Allow… to turn on Accessibility for Mirror Notch.
  3. Copy something on your iPhone, open Mirror Notch and flick the top edge away from you. Your Mac pastes it where you're typing.

To paste an item from the shelf, swipe it to the right. If nothing on the Mac is ready for typing, the item waits on the Mac's clipboard and in the notch: press ⌘V where you want it. So that iOS doesn't ask before every swing, set Settings → Mirror Notch → Paste from Other Apps to Allow.

Can I paste several things at once?

Yes. Send several things from your iPhone: drag a few photos into Mirror Notch, use its Paste button or the share sheet, or copy several photos at once and open Mirror Notch. One swing then pastes them all on your Mac. A swing pastes everything sent since the last swing, up to five minutes apart. Copying something else on either device starts over: then the swing pastes the newest thing.

In the notch, drag a box around several items to select them, then drag them out together or click one of them to copy them all. The shelf keeps your last 20 items; open the rest with the button at the end of the row.

macOS asks whether to open the app

Mirror Notch for Mac is notarized by Apple. The first time you open an app downloaded from the internet, macOS asks you to confirm. Choose Open. Move the app to Applications before you open it.

My iPhone doesn't find my Mac

  • Check that both devices are on the same Wi‑Fi network and that Mirror Notch is running on the Mac.
  • On your iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on Mirror Notch. Do the same under Bluetooth.
  • On a Mac with macOS 15 or later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on Mirror Notch. Without it, no iPhone can find the Mac.

Mirror Notch isn't in the Dynamic Island

  • On your iPhone, open Settings → Mirror Notch and make sure Live Activities is on.
  • iOS ends a Live Activity after eight hours. Open Mirror Notch to start it again.

Photos copied on my iPhone don't stay on the Mac shelf

macOS keeps photos from Universal Clipboard in a protected folder. In Mirror Notch's settings on the Mac, under Shelf, choose Allow Full Disk Access… and turn on Mirror Notch.

How do I unpair an iPhone?

On the Mac, open the gear in the notch, then iPhones → Forget. On the iPhone, Forget this Mac in Mirror Notch does the same from the other side. Either way, the two have to pair again before they can connect.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your iPhone and Mac talk to each other directly, and nothing you send between them goes to the developer or to any server. The only other request is the Mac app checking this website once a day for a new version, and it carries nothing about you. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
